median over sites: FREE probe 0.815 A   vs   REAL backbone 0.763 A (5 histidines)

THE REAL BACKBONE DOES NOT HOLD ITS HISTIDINES ANY TIGHTER: 1.07x, against
a 1.5x bar set before the numbers. The free probe at 0.815 A sits
INSIDE the spread of the five engineered histidines on a real VHH framework
(0.536-1.283 A).

So s10's collapse is NOT a fixture artifact, and this was the last cheap way out.
A histidine on a real scaffold is about as free to rotate away from a cation as
our unattached tripeptide was. s4 prices two anchor sites on the opposite
assumption.
